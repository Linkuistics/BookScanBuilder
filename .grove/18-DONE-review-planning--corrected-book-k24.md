### 1. High — the staff-trace trial cannot satisfy the library's own validity pre-steps on page 140 by layout, so its predictable outcome is a "rejected" label with the wrong cause

**Contract.** `k20:33–38` keeps "default validity checks" and the "fifteen-long-line
minimum" and makes guide acquisition the *only* independent variable;
`k20:46–53` forbids a lowered minimum or forced-valid fit; `k20:68–72` says
"failure of the unchanged validity rules rejects that configured candidate".
`k21:24–30, 59–63` consume "rejected" and, for it, offer only interpolation or a
two-axis mapping.

**Defect.** In Leptonica 1.87.0 validity is a chain of pre-steps in
`dewarpBuildPageModel` (`lept:178–235`), not one rule: (1) drop every line shorter
than 0.8× the longest (`lept:197`; `wrap:31` reproduces it for diagnostics),
(2) at least `minlines` lines (`lept:214`; `wrap:46` sets 15), and (3)
`dewarpIsLineCoverageValid` (`lept:223`, defined `1258–1322`): at least four
lines in each page half **and** first-point extent spanning more than 50% of page
height. Only then does `dewarpFindVertDisparity` run (`lept:235`, `301`), which
contains none of the three. `k20` names (2); `note:86` records (1); (3) appears
in neither.

On the unchanged page-140 render (1720 × 2459) there are six systems: Ex.101 at
the top and the final Ex.102 system are short — rules measure about 740 px and
about 870 px — while the four middle systems are about 1,390–1,400 px. Both
short systems are under 0.8× the longest (≈0.53 and ≈0.62), so (1) removes them
from any fit. The remaining full-width rules span y≈780 to y≈1,820, about 42% of
the height, under the 50% of (3). I checked this against `music-marked.png`, the
frozen record's evaluation lines (`ex101-top` and `system5-top` sit in exactly the
two short systems), and k4's actual detected fit (four lines, all at y 813–1410).
So a complete and perfect trace of every rule on this page still ends *invalid*.
The wrapper has no trace-input path today: `wrap:66` calls
`dewarpBuildPageModel`, which runs its own extraction, and the only exported
entry that accepts supplied traces is `dewarpFindVertDisparity`
(`/opt/homebrew/include/leptonica/allheaders.h:681`), which silently skips (1)–(3).
Using it makes "unchanged validity" false by omission; honouring them predicts
failure on this fixture. `k20:28–29` ("check the actual trace-input path") is
the right instinct but sits in Context, not in a falsifiable Done-when.

**Consequence.** A human tracing session plus a human reaction would be spent to
produce an invalid result whose cause is fixture layout against a library
prerequisite, and `k20:70–71` would label it a rejected configured candidate.
That contradicts `k20:50–53` ("precise gap/inconclusive") and hands `k21` a
"rejected" core whose supported next questions (sampler, two-axis) the evidence
does not touch — the premature expansion the root forbids.

**Smallest correction.** Make a coordinate-only pre-flight the first Done-when
item, run on the frozen traces *before any output is viewed*: line count after
the longest-line filter, per-half counts, vertical coverage fraction. Name which
library pre-steps apply to supplied traces and which entry point the wrapper
uses. Pre-declare that an unmet prerequisite is a fixture/guide-coverage gap
(inconclusive, naming the prerequisite), and reserve "rejected" for a model built
under the stated pre-steps that then fails curvature, consistency or quality
checks. If the pre-flight fails on page 140, the next decision is a fixture
choice (a music page whose full-width systems cover enough height) or an
explicit, separately named policy change — not a forced fit. Recommended: choose
a fixture rather than relax a validity rule, because the root says to report
unsupported cases rather than silently relax quality requirements.

### 2. Medium — whole-rule holdout drops k4's exclusion-zone separation, so held-out rules can be near-duplicates of fitted neighbours

**Contract.** `k20:46–53`: hold out "whole physical evaluation lines across
their full extent", supply only independent fitting traces, no duplicate traces
or borrowed points.

**Defect.** k4 separated fit from evaluation spatially: every evaluation line's
full extent sat inside a rectangular exclusion and "nearby rules can be removed
too" (`note:80–86`). `k20` replaces that with a per-rule unit. On this page the
five rules of one staff are about 12 px apart (measured rows ≈403, 413, 425, 436,
450 in Ex.101), and `dewarpFindVertDisparity` reduces each rule to one quadratic
(`lept:339`) before interpolating across rules. A held-out rule with four
fitted neighbours within ≈12–50 px is nearly determined by them; its residual
measures near-duplicate interpolation, not the extrapolation `k20:58–60` says to
inspect. No clause makes the evaluation unit lie away from fitted traces.

**Consequence.** An "improvement over rigid deskew" can be produced by locality
alone, which is the false-support case `k20:46–48` is trying to exclude.

**Smallest correction.** Define the evaluation unit as a whole staff or system
(all of its rules), record each unit's distance to the nearest fitted trace, keep
at least one unit per upper/middle/lower band where the fixture permits, and
carry k4's separation overlays forward. No numeric threshold is needed — the
distances are reported, not graded.

### 3. Medium — no outcome class or noise estimate for "improvement within trace uncertainty"

**Contract.** `k20:54–61` measures held-out slope/curvature against the rigid
baseline; `k20:68–70` rejects when there is "no useful held-out improvement".

**Defect.** The headroom over rigid is small on this fixture. After rigid deskew
the four held-out music rules still rise +2.60, +1.59, −2.41 and −0.22 px
end-to-end with curvature peak-to-peak 0.65, 1.27, 0.71 and 1.34 px (report
`pages.music.lines[0..3].rigid`); marking uncertainty is "roughly ±1 pixel on
unobscured rules, ±2 near notation" (`note:75`), and k4's own note calls the
curvature "close to marking uncertainty" (`note:108–109`). `k20` adds new traces
made at that same precision but offers only supported/rejected and
inconclusive-for-human-reason (`k20:71–72`); an effect that is indistinguishable
from the tracing uncertainty has no class and no estimate of that uncertainty
(the ±1/±2 is asserted in k4, not measured).

**Consequence.** A real improvement and a null look the same, and the trial gets
filed as "no useful improvement → rejected" (`k20:68–70`), steering `k21` toward
a sampler or two-axis question that a below-noise result does not justify.

**Smallest correction.** Add a repeatability measurement (independently re-trace
a subset, or a second pass, to measure trace noise rather than assume it); add an
explicit inconclusive class "difference not distinguishable at the measured trace
uncertainty", distinct from both support and rejection; report the held-out
improvement against that measured figure. No invented threshold.
