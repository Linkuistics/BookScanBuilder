# staff-acquisition-k27


## Goal

Test whether fresh manual staff marking from exact native pixel patches can
provide admissible dense source traces and measured repeatability on the
unchanged selected music page. Deliver source observations, blind repeats,
unchanged native preflight and inspected overlays, with explicit human
dispositions. Generate no fitted model or corrected image in this session.

## Done when

- State the hypothesis before marking: exact source-pixel addressing and
  unobscured native patches can remove the wide-strip centre/occlusion errors
  exposed by k26, while retaining sufficient physical guides after the same
  holdouts. This tests manual observation feasibility, not automatic extraction
  or correction quality. Reject the attempted acquisition if source checks
  reveal invented, obscured or outside-rule observations; absent/ambiguous
  centres, inadequate coverage or unmeasurable repeatability are precise
  inconclusive outcomes. Do not increase the old ±3-pixel assertion to admit it.
- Recheck source/render hashes and complete canvas. Start a genuinely fresh
  first pass from unmarked source patches. Native 1:1 pixels or labelled
  nearest-neighbour integer zoom must preserve exact pixel-centre addressing;
  retain each inspected patch's source rectangle, scale, identity and origin.
  No bicubic strip approximation, transfer of rejected y-values, pixel snapping,
  automatic centre extraction, interpolated gap or assumed five-line spacing
  may masquerade as a manually observed physical-rule point.
- Retain individual physical-rule identities and fit/evaluation roles, with
  source observations across upper/middle/lower and left/centre/right content.
  Declare dense sampling and obscured-gap handling before output: mark actual
  rule centres where visible, retain omitted/ambiguous columns and reasons,
  and distinguish display connectors from observations. Do not invent end
  points through notation or shorten long rules merely to pass filtering.
  Retain the prior whole-staff/system evaluation units A/E/H and their complete
  exclusion rectangles from the frozen record, without weakening or moving
  them to salvage coverage. Reconfirm their physical extent on the unchanged
