# page-review-k47

## Goal

Deliver an installed sole-PDF page reviewer: complete-page import/assessment,
any-page inspection and exact right-angle orientation, durable accept/revert/
resume and acknowledged image-only export. This is useful without fractional
edits, splitting or book placement.

## Context

Read k44 and its actual planning review/integration, k41/k45/k46, current v2
spec and both ADRs. The root's **Reviewed-design working slices** contract and
acceptance/retirement policy bind this leaf in full. Consume preparation's spec,
acceptance and optional-hint meaning; no experiment is a production processor.

Candidate seams are `bookscan.cli.run`, `prepare.operate`,
`pdf.document_info/project_page` and `publication.Staged`. Re-read exact source
through graph/coverage before sharing support gates or publication. Preparation's
graph copier is not an image exporter. Extend CLI routing without changing any
preparation invocation/help/error. Reconcile the existing staging responsibility's
preparation-specific names/errors without exposing protocol or regressing its
commit/cancellation tests. The current product depends directly on pikepdf
10.16.0; Pillow resolves 12.3.0 transitively, while historical experiments used
11.3.0. A direct Pillow pin and packaged version-aware estimator bridge belong
to this working importer/assessment, not a setup-only leaf. k48 consumes this
handoff; k28 owns pending product reactions and full-default gaps after k27.

  static PDFs. Freeze/verify a private source snapshot before serving; every
  occurrence has immutable complete 300-dpi RGB baseline, stable ID, physical
  geometry, provenance and accepted state. Resume after source disappearance.
  Ordinary PDFs and unavailable/malformed hints work; metadata restores no review.

  angle/status/ratio/settings/regions and explicit unavailable/non-finite/zero-
  confidence outcomes. Orientation-unverified, source-fidelity/extent and actual

  tiny/noisy/nonzero estimate is applied. Fractional/crop/split/group controls
  are visibly unsupported at this stage; possible spreads remain whole.

  preview/accept/discard/revert durably permutes pixels exactly at preserved
  scale. Show original CropBox and separately labelled source-native detail

  displayed-revision actions, corruption/stale diagnostics, authenticated server,
