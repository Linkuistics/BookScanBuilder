## Goal
Deliver a complete installed sole-PDF baseline reviewer: full-page RGB snapshot/
assessment, any-page source/full/native inspection, durable unresolved dispositions
and acknowledgement, project resume, normal Stop and image-only unchanged-baseline
export. A book is inspectable/exportable without any future geometry-edit stage.
