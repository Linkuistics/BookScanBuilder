  actually cuts; k21 has no descendants. Retain and verify unresolved music,
  preparation and every representative-case obligation even if an independent
  gutter result is accepted. Trials/source-only acquisition cannot replace the
  working processor or this root-wide integrated acceptance.

  public commands. Prepare explicit real selections from Raw Scans, Processed
  Books and Needing Cleanup with assigned covers, retaining each source's
  digest. Stage the prepared PDF outside the collection, then run the processor
  with collection folders unavailable through an observed access boundary.
  A different COLLECTION string alone proves no isolation. Process an ordinary
  unmarked already-assembled PDF too; no required sidecar or rediscovered source.
- Use a bounded labelled synthetic assembly and the root's real-fixture set:
  music 40/140 plus Schoenberg cover, Norwegian spread 20, Counterpoint 20,
  Algorithm Design 20 and explicit multi-page front/back cover roles. Retain
  full-page/native-detail outputs, exact commands/order, roles/provenance,
  source/prepared/final digests and before/after run subjects. Human preparation
  approval must have a separate inspected-view response; if still pending or
  rejected, obtain/repair it rather than marking workflow accepted.
- Demonstrate within-page geometry and border removal with preserved colour,
  faint text, music and illustrations; already-correct pages are not degraded.
  Inspect both Norwegian halves and retain Counterpoint as one book page.
  Exercise flagged review plus adjustment of an otherwise unflagged page, and
  show the accepted final image in the exported PDF.
- A real/synthetic spread fixture includes a blank half and a duplicated half.
  Show default provenance/order/no silent deletion, then explicit user exclusion
  or movement at book-page granularity, and verify final order, retained image
  geometry and aligned English OCR after those accepted decisions.
- Verify actual English text search and selection positions on the final PDF,
  including gutter halves, a manually adjusted page and an earlier-OCR fixture.
  Earlier text must not produce stale/duplicate misaligned selection. Compare
  final visible images to the accepted images: OCR cannot silently rotate,
  recolour or substitute them. Preserve every original on success/failure.
- Verify optional whitening/noise removal at its shipped preview/apply/revert
  seam against faint-print, music, illustration and colour companions. Default
  disabled behaviour remains useful; applied/reverted decisions affect final
  images and aligned OCR as designed. Record the user's visual judgment rather
  than deriving acceptance from numerical quality or silence.
- Run new/changed and affected existing Taskfile recipes, including preparation
  command checks. Freeze subjects before/after with one writer and no mid-run
  edits, retain per-item results, and publish a durable workflow acceptance note
  with ordinary comparison paths and independently recorded human responses.
  If any root criterion is unmet, cut bounded repair/completion and a fresh
  acceptance consumer before retiring; do not close root on a partial handoff.
